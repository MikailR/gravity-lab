Physics

6,000 massless tracers follow two moving gravity sources. Softened Newtonian acceleration and leapfrog integration use fixed time steps. Tracers do not attract one another. Units are arbitrary.

In plain words

The bright cores pull the stars along curved paths. Your pointer adds another pull. These are evolving trajectories, not a recorded animation.
